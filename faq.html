Appointments

How much is the initial consultation?

Consultations are $99, in-office or by telehealth when appropriate. This covers a medical evaluation and discussion of personalized options whether or not you begin treatment.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Consultations are available in-office or by telehealth. Ask the practice whether telehealth is available for your service and location.

Services & labs

What services do you offer?

Services include medical weight management, bioidentical hormone therapy, hormone balancing, rheumatology and arthritis care, PRP, peptide therapy, vitamin support, and diagnostic lab testing. See the services page for more details.

What laboratory services are available?

Labcorp testing is available. Your provider can discuss which tests may be appropriate for your evaluation and care.

How much do labs cost, and when are results ready?

Testing costs and result timing depend on the specific test. Eligible Labcorp testing may be billed to insurance. Confirm costs and timing before testing.

Do you offer vitamin injections?

The practice lists Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, Lipomino, Tri-Immune, and NAD+ options. Ask your provider whether a particular option is available and appropriate for you.

Payment

Do you accept insurance?

NeXtGen Medical is a private-pay clinic and does not accept insurance for services or treatments. Eligible Labcorp testing may be billed to insurance.

What payment methods do you accept?

The practice lists major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, and HSA/FSA cards when applicable. Payment is due at the time of service.

Do I have to start treatment after my consultation?

No. The consultation covers your evaluation and discussion of options; you decide whether to begin a treatment plan.

Are payment plans available?

Financing options may be available depending on your treatment plan. Contact the practice for current details.
