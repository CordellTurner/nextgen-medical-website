01 / THE SERVICE

Rheumatology support for complex care.

Rheumatology care addresses arthritis and other complex conditions involving joints and inflammation. At NeXtGen Medical, Claudia continues to provide rheumatology care alongside other services offered by the practice.

02 / WHO IT MAY BE FOR

Bring your questions and health history.

Patients seeking care for arthritis or chronic and complex concerns can contact the practice to discuss whether an appointment is a fit. The consultation helps the provider understand your history and concerns and consider appropriate next steps.

03 / WHAT TO EXPECT

Discuss evaluation and care options.

At an initial consultation, your provider reviews your medical history and talks with you about your concerns. Laboratory testing or other follow-up may be discussed if appropriate. Specific treatment decisions and follow-up depend on your evaluation.

04 / COST & CONSULTATION

Get started with a consultation.

The initial consultation is $99, in-office or by telehealth. The fee applies whether or not you begin treatment. Ask the practice about any additional visit, testing, or treatment costs relevant to your care.
