01 / THE SERVICES

What PRP and joint options are offered?

Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) is prepared from a patient's own blood. The practice lists PRP services for hair restoration, joint injections, and facials. Joint care may also include other injection options, depending on your evaluation and the service discussed with your provider.

02 / WHO IT MAY BE FOR

Consider options with a provider.

If you have a joint, hair, or skin concern, you can ask whether services are available and appropriate for you. Your provider reviews your medical history, concerns, and goals. Not every service is suitable for every patient, and individual results vary.

03 / WHAT TO EXPECT

Know the process before treatment.

At your consultation, discuss your concern, the proposed service, potential risks and benefits, alternatives, expected follow-up, and costs. Your provider can explain preparation and aftercare for a specific procedure if it is appropriate for you.

04 / COST & CONSULTATION

Discuss costs up front.

The initial consultation is $99, in-office or by telehealth. The fee applies whether or not you begin treatment. Procedure and treatment pricing is not listed here; ask the practice for current costs.
