01 / AVAILABLE OPTIONS

Vitamin & wellness shots.

The practice lists Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, Lipomino, Tri-Immune, and NAD+ among its wellness shot and injection services. These options are discussed in the context of your individual health and wellness needs. Availability and suitability can vary.

Vitamin D

Ask your provider whether Vitamin D injections are appropriate for you based on your individual needs.

Vitamin B12

Discuss Vitamin B12 injections as one possible part of individualized wellness care.

Lipomino

Ask the practice about Lipomino injections and whether they may be appropriate for your care.

Tri-Immune

Tri-Immune injections are offered as part of personalized wellness services.

NAD+

NAD+ is listed by the practice as an IM/SQ wellness service. Ask your provider about the specific option and whether it is appropriate for you.

02 / IS IT RIGHT FOR ME?

It starts with your needs.

People interested in these services can ask questions during a consultation. Your provider considers your medical history and individual circumstances before discussing options. No injection is right for everyone.

03 / WHAT TO EXPECT

Review options with your provider.

Your provider can explain the available service, discuss relevant health considerations, and answer questions about what to expect. Do not assume a treatment is suitable without an individual evaluation.

04 / COST & CONSULTATION

Start with a consultation.

The initial consultation is $99, in-office or by telehealth. The fee applies whether or not you begin treatment. Individual injection pricing is not listed here; contact the practice for current costs.
