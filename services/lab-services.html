Lab services, made convenient.

01 / THE SERVICE

Testing through Labcorp.

NeXtGen Medical offers lab services through Labcorp. Depending on your reason for seeking care, your provider may discuss whether testing could be useful as part of your evaluation or follow-up.

02 / WHAT MAY BE AVAILABLE

Testing depends on your care needs.

Your provider can explain which laboratory tests are available and relevant to your health concerns. Test selection is individualized; this site does not specify a comprehensive test list or guarantee that a particular test is offered.

03 / RESULTS & FOLLOW-UP

Review results with your provider.

Your provider can discuss the purpose of any recommended testing, how results will be reviewed, and appropriate next steps. Ask the practice about expected timing and any preparation instructions for a specific test.

04 / COST & INSURANCE

Ask about test pricing.

The $99 consultation fee applies to your initial in-office or telehealth visit. Eligible Labcorp testing may be billed to insurance. Coverage, eligibility, and out-of-pocket costs can vary; confirm test pricing with the practice and Labcorp before testing.
