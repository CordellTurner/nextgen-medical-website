01 / THE SERVICE

What is medical weight management?

Medical weight management is an opportunity to discuss your goals with a medical provider and consider whether supervised care may be appropriate for you. NeXtGen Medical lists semaglutide and tirzepatide among its weight-management options. Medication is not appropriate for everyone, and a consultation does not guarantee a prescription.

02 / WHO IT MAY BE FOR

Start with an individual evaluation.

People interested in clinical support for weight-related health goals can discuss whether this service fits their needs. Your provider considers your medical history, current health, medications, and goals before discussing options. Eligibility is determined individually.

03 / WHAT TO EXPECT

A plan built around your care.

Your initial visit includes a medical evaluation and discussion of personalized options. Lab testing may be considered when appropriate. If a treatment plan is started, your provider will discuss follow-up and monitoring. The exact plan and schedule depend on your individual evaluation.

04 / COST & CONSULTATION

Clear about the first step.

The initial consultation is $99, in-office or by telehealth. The fee applies whether or not you begin treatment. Medication and ongoing treatment costs are not listed here; contact the practice for current pricing before making a decision.
