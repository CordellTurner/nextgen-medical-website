Claudia Moulden, ANP-C, MSN

Claudia Moulden, ANP-C, MSN, is the founder and lead provider of NeXtGen Medical. A board-certified Adult Nurse Practitioner with three nursing degrees, she began her career in neurology, helping establish the first stroke center in Niagara County, New York.

She has practiced rheumatology for over 13 years and continues to care for patients with arthritis and complex conditions. Claudia has several years of hormonal health experience, completed The Hormonal Health Institute’s hormone therapy education and certification program, and has additional peptide therapy training. Her approach is collaborative, evidence-based, and tailored to each person’s needs and goals.

Appointments

How much do appointments cost?

The initial consultation is $99, in-office or by telehealth. Rates for other appointments and services vary; check the online scheduler or call the practice to confirm current pricing.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Consultations are available in-office or by telehealth. Ask the practice about availability and whether telehealth is appropriate for the care you are seeking.

Labs

What laboratory services are available?

Labcorp testing is available. Your provider can discuss which testing may be appropriate for your care.

How much do labs cost, and when are results ready?

Testing costs and result timing depend on the specific test. Eligible Labcorp testing may be billed to insurance. Contact the practice or Labcorp to confirm costs and expected timing before testing.

Telehealth

Which services and states are available by telehealth?

Availability depends on the service and where you are located. Contact the practice to confirm whether telehealth is available for your needs and location.

Services

What services do you offer?

Services include medical weight management, hormone therapy, rheumatology care, PRP, joint treatments, peptides, NAD+, vitamin injections, and lab testing. Explore the services page for more details.

Do you offer vitamin injections?

The practice lists Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, Lipomino, and Tri-Immune injections. Ask your provider whether an option is appropriate for you.

Payment

Do you accept insurance?

NeXtGen Medical is a private-pay clinic and does not accept insurance for services or treatments. Eligible Labcorp testing may be billed to insurance.

What payment methods do you accept?

Major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, and HSA/FSA cards when applicable. Payment is due at the time of service.

What is the cancellation or refund policy?

Policies can depend on the appointment or service. Contact the practice directly for current cancellation and refund terms before booking or purchasing a treatment.

Do I have to start treatment after my consultation?

No. Your provider will discuss appropriate options and any associated costs with you before you decide whether to proceed with treatment.

Are payment plans available?

Financing options may be available depending on your treatment plan. The team can review what's available during your consultation.
