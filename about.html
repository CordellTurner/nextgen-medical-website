Claudia Moulden, ANP-C, MSN

Claudia Moulden, ANP-C, MSN, is a Board-Certified Adult Nurse Practitioner and the founder and sole provider of NeXtGen Medical LLC. With more than 30 years of experience in healthcare, Claudia brings a broad clinical background and a personalized approach to the care she provides to her patients.

Claudia began her healthcare career in 1996, gaining experience in multiple areas of medicine, including neurology and rheumatology. In 2010, she served as a Stroke Clinical Coordinator and played an important role in helping establish the first designated stroke center in Niagara County, New York. This experience helped shape her commitment to comprehensive, patient-centered care and the importance of looking beyond individual symptoms when evaluating a patient's health.

In 2012, Claudia became a Board-Certified Adult Nurse Practitioner. Since then, she has continued to expand her clinical knowledge and develop an approach to care that combines her years of medical experience with individualized evaluation and treatment.

Peptide & Hormone Therapy
Since 2018, Claudia has pursued specialized education and hands-on experience in peptide therapy, bioidentical hormone replacement therapy (BHRT), and hormone health through the Hormone Health Institute. Her extensive experience in these areas allows her to provide individualized, medically supervised treatment for patients seeking hormone and peptide-based therapies.

Weight Management
Claudia provides medically supervised weight management for both men and women. Treatment plans are individualized based on each patient's health history, goals, and medical needs, with ongoing monitoring, education, and support throughout treatment.

Rheumatology & Regenerative Treatments
With a strong background in rheumatology, Claudia also provides PRP and regenerative treatments for appropriate joint and soft-tissue conditions. Her rheumatology experience allows her to take a broader approach when evaluating patients with joint, muscle, neck, or back concerns. When clinically appropriate, she may also evaluate for potential underlying inflammatory or rheumatologic conditions that could be contributing to a patient's symptoms.

A Patient-Centered Approach
As the sole provider at NeXtGen Medical, Claudia is directly involved in each patient's evaluation and treatment. She believes in taking the time to understand each patient's health concerns, history, and individual needs rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach.

Her goal is to provide knowledgeable, personalized medical care while helping patients better understand their health and treatment options.

Outside of Medicine
Outside of her work in medicine, Claudia enjoys spending quality time with her husband and their two daughters. She is also a proud dog mom to two Yorkies and a Doberman. In her free time, she enjoys discovering new coffee shops, traveling to new cities, and experiencing the local culture and food each destination has to offer. She also enjoys returning to her hometown of Buffalo, New York, from time to time to reconnect with family, friends, and the place she will always consider home.
